# Crimechester - a game by Katie Wunderle
# I wanted to make my own neopets and I did.
# Thank you Dave Gray for your videos that helped the login form make sense

Online gaming and interactive communities have been a cornerstone of the evolution of the internet. Browser based games gave users immersive environments, customizable features, and vibrant communities. With the decommissioning of Flash in 2020 because of security vulnerabilities and modern web standards, we saw the end of many interactive websites and the rise of applications filled with microtransactions and lacking in narrative and user interaction. Mysteries and detective stories have captivated audiences with interesting storylines and the ability to make the viewer feel like they are a part of solving a crime.

It has been a dream of mine to build a browser based web application in the vein of things like Neopets, Wizard Mansion, Gaia Online, and Kingdom of Loathing, among many others. The late 90's and early 2000's internet was a colorful wonderland that was just coming into popularity and it has since revolutionized how our society interacts, along with transforming the way we view pop culture and nostalgia. 

I present Crimechester, an interactive role playing game where the user can be and feel like a detective. Through creative storytelling, case management, evidence collection and analysis, and user interaction, players can piece together the clues to solve the mystery.
